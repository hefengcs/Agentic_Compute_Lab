Toward Self-Evolving Agent Harnesses

Our Research Agenda | October 2026

Beyond Static Agent Systems

Recent advances in large language models have substantially expanded the capabilities of AI agents. Yet, an agent's effectiveness depends not only on its underlying foundation model, but also on how it interacts with tools, manages information, executes tasks, and learns from experience.

These external execution mechanisms, commonly referred to as Agent Harnesses, are still largely designed and optimized through human engineering.

We believe there is an exciting opportunity to move beyond static, manually engineered agent systems toward self-evolving agent harnesses.

Rather than treating an agent's execution framework as a fixed component, we are interested in understanding how these mechanisms can be automatically discovered, adapted, and improved through experience.

Our Research Interests

Our exploration centers on three interconnected questions.

1. Learning from Experience

How can agents accumulate, organize, and reuse their experiences to support continuous improvement?

We are particularly interested in understanding what makes experience useful for future adaptation, and how agents can benefit from increasingly complex histories of previous interactions and decisions.

2. Understanding Self-Improvement

What determines an agent's ability to improve its own execution mechanisms?

We seek to better understand the conditions under which self-improvement succeeds or fails, and whether these capabilities can be extended to a broader range of agent systems.

3. Scalable Agent Evolution

How can automated agent evolution become more efficient, scalable, and generalizable?

We are interested in approaches that reduce the cost of experimentation, support sustained improvement, and enable discoveries to transfer across tasks and environments.

Looking Ahead

We view self-evolving agent harnesses as a promising direction at the intersection of agentic AI, automated optimization, and recursive self-improvement.

Our goal is to develop a deeper understanding of how agent systems can improve beyond their initial human-designed configurations.

We look forward to sharing insights from this exploration as our research progresses.

We welcome conversations and collaborations with researchers interested in the future of self-evolving AI agents.
